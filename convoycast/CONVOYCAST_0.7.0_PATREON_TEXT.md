# ConvoyCast 0.7.0

## Open events for everyone

ConvoyCast is an experimental application for discovering, creating, and
sharing ATS and ETS2 events.

Many community events are scattered across Discord servers, private groups,
and other platforms. If you are not in the right place, you may never hear
about them. ConvoyCast is an attempt to make these events easier to discover
and more accessible.

The Desktop version is mainly for organizers who want to create, publish, and
share events. The Mobile version is primarily an event reader for discovering
activities and checking their details.

ConvoyCast is open, independent, and decentralized. It does not rely on a
central server collecting and redistributing every event, nor on a permanent
moderation team or a single authority deciding what the community can see.
Users choose which people, communities, and event lists to follow, trust,
block, or ignore. CONVOYRAMA maintains the software, but does not control the
network or the events users share.

ConvoyCast works best when people collaborate with each other. An event needs
at least one other participant on the network in order to be shared. Users
share events directly with one another, helping the network grow through
cooperation.

## Experimental release

ConvoyCast is still under development. It may contain bugs, behave
unexpectedly, or be incomplete in some areas. Suggestions and bug reports are
very welcome, and they help us improve the application.
